What TechBridge is

TechBridge, by Baselink Services Limited, exists to bridge learning to real-world experience. We help learners move from studying a skill to using it.

Our programs build practical skills in Data Analytics and Web Development, and the 30-day practical internship gives you a place to apply them.

  1. Learning
  2. Practical programs
  3. Real-world experience

  • What we offer

    Programs in Data Analytics and Web Development, and a 30-day practical internship.

  • Who it is for

    Interns and learners who want hands-on experience.

  • Why join

    You learn through practical, project-based work, and strong performance may earn you a recommendation letter.
