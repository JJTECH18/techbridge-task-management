Internship Tasks

TechBridge provides a structured 30-day practical internship. You build your web development skills step by step by completing 8 practical projects, with the work getting more challenging as you progress.

See the journey Apply for TechBridge Internship

What the internship involves

Each task introduces a new piece of the TechBridge platform. You start by building a website with HTML and CSS, and finish by combining everything into a complete internship platform.

The 8 tasks
